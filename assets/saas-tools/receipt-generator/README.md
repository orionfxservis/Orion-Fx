## 🎨 Customizable Receipt Templates

Receipt Generator is designed around flexible receipt templates.

Businesses can customize important elements of their receipt, including:

- Company logo
- Company name
- Business information
- Customer information
- Product/service details
- Receipt number
- Date
- Payment information
- Totals
- Terms & conditions
- Footer information

The template system allows branding elements such as the **logo and company name** to fit within defined areas of the receipt while maintaining a professional layout.

## ⚡ Simple Workflow

### 1. Enter Business Information

Add your:

- Company name
- Logo
- Address
- Phone number
- Email
- Other business information

### 2. Add Customer Information

Enter customer details where required.

### 3. Add Products or Services

Add products or services with:

- Description
- Quantity
- Unit price
- Amount

Totals are calculated automatically.

### 4. Add Payment Information

Enter payment details, discounts, taxes, and other applicable information.

### 5. Add Terms & Conditions

Add your business terms, return policy, payment conditions, or other notes.

### 6. Preview Your Receipt

Review the receipt before generating the final document.

### 7. Export or Print

Generate your receipt as:

- PDF
- JPG/Image
- Printed document
