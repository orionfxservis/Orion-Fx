🧾 Professional Business Documents in Minutes

Our first SaaS application, Orion Fx Receipt Generator, makes it easy to create professional receipts online.

Enter your company name, upload your business logo, add customer information, enter products or services, specify quantities and prices, add discounts or taxes, include payment information and terms & conditions, and instantly generate a professional receipt.

Receipts can be downloaded as PDF or JPG, printed, or shared digitally, making the tool useful for shops, retailers, freelancers, service providers, restaurants, repair businesses & other small businesses.
