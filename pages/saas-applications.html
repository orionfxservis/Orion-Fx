❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using Orion Fx online SaaS applications.

Are Orion Fx SaaS tools completely free to use?

Yes. Both the Receipt Generator and InvoiceCraft (Invoice Generator) are 100% free with no trial limits, hidden subscription charges, or mandatory watermarks on your exported PDF documents.

Is my business and client data secure?

Yes. All tools operate entirely client-side inside your own browser. Your customer names, rates, invoices, and payment credentials are never sent to or stored on our servers.

Can I print on thermal receipt printers as well as A4?

Yes. The Receipt Generator features built-in thermal print styles for standard 58mm and 80mm POS receipt printers, while InvoiceCraft is formatted for standard A4 and Letter paper.

Can I send documents directly to clients on WhatsApp?

Yes. Both applications include 1-click WhatsApp sharing that creates a pre-formatted, polite message with key totals and invoice details ready to send to your client's phone number.

Can I use Pakistani Rupees (PKR) and foreign currencies?

Yes. The applications support Pak Rs. (PKR) as standard, as well as USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SAR, and more, complete with automatic live conversion rates and multi-currency tracking.

Do individual tools have their own feature overviews?

Yes. You can explore deep-dive guides, screenshots, and step-by-step instructions on our dedicated Receipt Generator Landing Page and InvoiceCraft Landing Page.
