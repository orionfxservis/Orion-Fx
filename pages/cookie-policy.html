1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, mobile phone or other device when you visit a website.

They help websites remember information about visitors, improve functionality, analyse website performance and support advertising services.

Some cookies are placed directly by our website, while others may be placed by third-party service providers.

2. How We Use Cookies

Orion Fx may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain essential website functionality.
  • Remember visitor preferences and settings.
  • Understand website traffic and visitor behaviour.
  • Analyse website performance and usability.
  • Improve website security and prevent misuse.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Understand the effectiveness of marketing campaigns.

The cookies used may vary depending on the services and features enabled on our website.

3. Types of Cookies

These cookies are necessary for certain website functions, security features and basic operations.

They help ensure that our website operates properly.

3.2 Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website.

They may collect information such as:

  • Website navigation and interactions.

We may use services such as Google Analytics for website analysis, if enabled.

3.3 Performance and Functional Cookies

These cookies help improve website performance, remember certain preferences and enhance the visitor experience.
