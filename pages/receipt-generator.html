❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about printing receipts, privacy, thermal printers, and supported export formats.

Is the Orion Fx Receipt Generator completely free?

Yes. You can generate unlimited receipts without paying any subscription fees or needing to register an account. There are no trial periods and no watermarks on your exported PDF documents.

Can I print directly to a thermal receipt printer?

Yes. The application has dedicated CSS @media print rules calibrated for standard 58mm and 80mm thermal receipt printers. When you click print, the system automatically formats the ticket for ESC/POS receipt paper without extra margins.

Is my customer and business data secure?

Yes, 100%. All processing and rendering takes place client-side within your browser. Your customer details, pricing, and business logo are never transmitted to our servers or saved in a remote database.

How can I share receipts on WhatsApp?

Click the "WhatsApp Share" button inside the tool. It formats the receipt ID, total balance, payment status, and verification URL into an orderly message and opens WhatsApp Web or your mobile WhatsApp application ready to send.

Can I add my company logo and return terms?

Yes. You can upload any PNG, JPG, or SVG store logo. You can also customize the footer notes to specify your business warranty terms, return policies, or social media links.
