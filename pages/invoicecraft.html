❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about InvoiceCraft templates, currency support, and data privacy.

Is InvoiceCraft really 100% free?

Yes. InvoiceCraft provides complete, unlimited access to all 6 templates, multi-currency features, and PDF exports without any trial limits, watermarks, or subscription fees.

How does multi-currency support work with PKR?

InvoiceCraft handles Pak Rs. (PKR) as a native currency with authentic Pakistani numeral formatting. It also supports USD, EUR, GBP, AED, and SAR with automatic currency conversions on your dashboard.

Will my client details and invoices be saved?

Yes! InvoiceCraft utilizes secure browser LocalStorage. When you return to the app, your saved profile, client directory, and past invoices are ready for you without needing an account.

Can I customize invoice colors and templates?

Yes. You can switch between 6 designer layouts (Corporate, Modern, Tech, Creative, Classic, Minimal) and choose from multiple brand accent colors to match your business style.

Is my business data kept secure and confidential?

Absolutely. All computations and storage happen entirely client-side on your device. Your financial records, client names, and invoice totals are never transmitted to external cloud servers.
